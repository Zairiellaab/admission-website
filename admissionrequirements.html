Admission Requirements

Academic Level Admission Requirements
Grade 1
(Integrated Laboratory School (ILS))

Minimum Requirements:

  • Age at least 6 years old by August 31 of the school year of enrollment
  • Must be able to identify alphabet and numbers
  • Undergone Kindergarten 2 from a DepEd-accredited school
  • Possess a Learner’s Reference Number (LRN)

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below 80 in all subjects during 1st and 2nd Quarters
  • General Average grade of 85 or better

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Kindergarten SF9 (front & back with all grades visible)
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from University Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished G1 Admission Form
Grade 7
(Junior High School)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Grade 6 student expected to graduate at the end of the current academic year

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below 80 in all subjects in Grade 5

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Grade 5 SF9 (front & back with all grades visible)
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from University Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished G7 Admission Form
Grade 11
(Senior High School)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Grade 10 student expected to graduate at the end of the current academic year

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below 85 in Mathematics and Science subjects in Grade 9
  • General Average grade of 85 or better

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Grade 9 SF9 (front & back with all grades visible)
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from University Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished G11 Admission Form
College Freshman
(Undergraduate Admissions)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Grade 12 student expected to graduate at the end of current school year; OR
  • SHS graduate from previous years who did not enroll in any other school after graduation; OR
  • ALS graduate certified eligible for tertiary level admission (or ALS graduate from 2019–2021)

Grade Requirements:

  • General Average of 85+ for programs with Board Examinations
  • General Average of 80+ for programs without Board Examinations
  • CEAT applicants: No grade below 85 in Mathematics and Science subjects

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Grade 11 SF9 (front & back with all grades visible)
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Accomplished College Admission Form

Special Privileges / Scholarship Credentials (if applicable):

  • NCIP Certification (Indigenous Peoples Group members)
College Transfer / 2nd Degree
(Transferees & Second-Degree Applicants)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Previously enrolled in another university for at least one semester; OR
  • Completed an undergraduate degree at an accredited college/university

Grade Requirements:

  • General Average of 2.25 or better for programs with Board Examinations
  • General Average of 2.50 or better for programs without Board Examinations

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Transcript of Records (TOR) or Certification of Grades from previous school
  • Certificate of GWA
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from University Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished College Admission Form
College Foreign Student
(International Applicants)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Full-fledged citizen of a foreign country; AND
  • Undergraduate student abroad wanting to transfer/continue at PSU; OR graduate of a foreign Senior High School

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below C+ in all subjects

Documentary Requirements:

  • Scholastic Records / Transcript of Records (showing all grades and GWA)
  • Honorable Dismissal (if college units were earned)
  • Passport Bio-page & Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR-I) Card
  • Student Visa (if applicable) & Police Clearance from country of origin
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school
  • Birth Certificate duly authenticated by Philippine Foreign Service Post (PFSP)
  • English Proficiency Certificate (IELTS-6 band score, TOEFL-min 500 paper / 173 computer) if non-English native/medium
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee (USD $17 / Php 800) from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
College Returning Student
(Readmission)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Previously admitted/attended PSU but has not enrolled for at least 10 consecutive semesters (5 consecutive years)

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Certification of Grades from OUR, duly evaluated by the College Program Chair
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Accomplished College Admission Form
Graduate School
(Master’s, Doctoral, & Diploma Programs)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Master’s Program: Baccalaureate degree holder with at least 9 units academic background in the applied discipline
  • Doctoral Program: Master’s degree holder with thesis and at least 12 academic units at master’s level in applied program

Grade Requirements:

  • Master’s Program: Undergraduate GWA of at least 2.0 or equivalent
  • Doctoral Program: Master’s GWA of at least 1.75 or equivalent

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate & Original Transcript of Records (TOR)
  • Transfer Credentials & 2 Reference Letters from previous employers/professors
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from University Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished Graduate School Admission Form
School of Law
(Juris Doctor Program)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Graduate of any baccalaureate degree program with required units: 18 units in English, 18 units in Social Sciences, and 6 units in Mathematics

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Transcript of Records (TOR)
  • Certificate of Honorable Dismissal (if non-PSU graduate)
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished School of Law Admission Form
School of Medicine
(Doctor of Medicine Program)

Minimum Requirements:

  • Graduate of any Bachelor of Science (BS) degree program
  • Taken the National Medical Admission Test (NMAT) within the last 2 years with a score not lower than the 40th percentile
  • Not currently enrolled in another medical school or a student transferee

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Original TOR covering at least first 3.5 years of Baccalaureate program
  • Original and photocopy of NMAT result
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from 2 college professors
  • 4 copies of 2x2 ID picture (signed on the front by applicant)
  • Original Certificate of Naturalization (for naturalized Filipino citizens issued by CID)
  • Latest Income Tax Returns (ITR) of both parents
  • DOST clearance (if recipient of DOST college scholarship)
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished SOM Admission Form
ETEEAP
(Expanded Tertiary Education Equivalency and Accreditation Program)

Minimum Requirements:

  • High School graduate or ALS qualifier
  • Employed for an aggregate of at least 5 years in a field/industry related to the applied academic discipline
  • At least 25 years old with proven capability/knowledge (Certificate of Employment)

Documentary Requirements:

  • Original copy of PSA Birth Certificate
  • Report Card / SF9 & 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)
  • Accomplished ETEEAP Admission Form
  • Work employment records and training summaries (public or private)
  • Summary Portfolio of: a) Trainings and Seminars attended (recent to old), b) Certificates of Proficiency (National Certificates), c) Other relevant supporting documents
