COLLEGE FOREIGN STUDENT

(International Applicants)

Foreign student applicants seeking admission to Palawan State University are required to submit the following documents for evaluation and processing:


  1. Pre-Admission Requirements – for compliance to PSU Admission and Testing Unit

    1. Accomplished Foreign Student Application Form
    2. Valid Passport
      • Photocopy of the biographical/data page
      • Passport must be valid for the required period of stay in the Philippines
    3. Birth Certificate or equivalent civil registry document
    4. Recent Passport-Size Photographs
    5. Academic Records
      • Original or authenticated copy of Transcript of Records/Academic Transcript
      • Diploma, Certificate of Graduation, or equivalent credential
    6. English Proficiency Certification, if applicable
    7. Certificate of Eligibility/Qualification for Admission, if required by the University
    8. Other documents required by the concerned college, program, or University Admission Office


  2. Immigration and Visa Requirements – To Be Submitted to the Institutional Student Program and Services (ISPS) Unit for Compliance Prior to Enrollment

    Foreign students are also required to submit the appropriate immigration documents, as applicable:


    1. Valid Passport
    2. Appropriate Philippine Student Visa/Permit or Visa Documentation
    3. Special Study Permit (SSP), when applicable
    4. Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR I-Card), when applicable
    5. Medical Examination Certificate from a Category III/Level III Hospital
    6. National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) Clearance
    7. Proof of Financial Capacity
    8. Affidavit of Support and Guarantee, when applicable
    9. Other documents prescribed by the Bureau of Immigration
    10. Authenticated or apostilled academic documents (authenticated/apostilled by the FS applicant’s Embassy)
    11. Official English translations of documents issued in languages other than English
    12. Authenticated or apostilled Certificate of Good Moral Character or equivalent (authenticated by the FS applicant’s Embassy)
    13. Recommendation or reference letter, when required
    14. Health or medical clearance
    15. Authenticated or apostilled Police or character clearance from the applicant's country of origin. (authenticated by the FS applicant’s Embassy)
    16. Proof of accommodation or local address in the Philippines
    17. Emergency contact information


Minimum Requirements:

  • Full-fledged citizen of a foreign country; AND
  • Undergraduate student abroad wanting to transfer/continue at PSU; OR graduate of a foreign Senior High School

Grade Requirements:

  • No grade below C+ in all subjects

Documentary Requirements:

  • Scholastic Records / Transcript of Records (showing all grades and GWA)
  • Honorable Dismissal (if college units were earned)
  • Passport Bio-page & Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR-I) Card
  • Student Visa (if applicable) & Police Clearance from country of origin
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school
  • Birth Certificate duly authenticated by Philippine Foreign Service Post (PFSP)
  • English Proficiency Certificate (IELTS-6 band score, TOEFL-min 500 paper / 173 computer) if non-English native/medium
  • 2x2 ID Picture
  • Official Receipt of Admission Fee (USD $17 / Php 800) from Cashier’s Office (non-refundable)

IMPORTANT REMINDERS

  • All submitted documents must be authentic, complete, valid, and properly authenticated or apostilled when required.
  • Documents issued in a language other than English should be accompanied by an official English translation.
  • Foreign student applicants should ensure that their immigration status and documents remain valid throughout their authorized stay in the Philippines.
  • Foreign Student Applicants are required to submit their complete Immigration and Visa Requirements in a long-sized black clear book to the Institutional Student Program and Services (ISPS) Unit for verification and compliance prior to enrollment.
  • Additional requirements may be requested depending on the applicant's nationality, educational qualifications, visa category, and intended program.
  • Applicants are advised to coordinate with the Palawan State University Office of Student Affairs and Services – International Student and Programs Services (ISPS) Unit and the appropriate University offices before completing their immigration transactions.
  • Submission of the required documents does not automatically constitute admission or approval of the applicant's visa or immigration status. Final approval remains subject to the applicable University policies and Philippine immigration regulations.
