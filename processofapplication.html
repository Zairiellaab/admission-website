2.2.1 INTEGRATED LABORATORY SCHOOL (ILS) ADMISSION PROCESS

Step Stage / Activity Detailed Description
Step 1 Registration Walk-in application and initial document submission at the ILS office.
Step 2 Initial Screening Screening and evaluation of initial academic and record credentials.
Step 3 Examination Administration of written, oral, and practical placement/admission examinations.
Step 4 Secondary Evaluation Comprehensive review and secondary evaluation of test performance and applicant credentials.
Step 5 Selection of Passers Scoring and ranking based on course quotas.
Step 6 Confirmation Period Applicants must log into the portal to accept or decline offers within 7 calendar days.
Step 7 List of Qualified Applicants Submission of official list of qualified applicants for enrollment to the Office of the Registrar.
